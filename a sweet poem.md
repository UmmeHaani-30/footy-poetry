# ⚽ Dreams on the Field - A Football Love Poem 💛

A beautiful, interactive handwritten poem combining football passion and romantic dreams under a starry night sky.

## ✨ Features
- Handwritten paper aesthetic with ink stains
- Starry night background with twinkling stars
- Subtle football field elements
- Interactive "Discover the Magic" button
- Floating hearts animation
- Fully responsive design

## 🎮 How to Use
1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Read the beautiful football-themed love poem
3. Click **"Discover the Magic"** to see floating hearts!
4. Hover over poem lines for subtle effects
