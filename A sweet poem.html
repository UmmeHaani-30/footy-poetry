Dreams on the Field

a handwritten note under the stars

I think of you in quiet ways,
Whisper your name before I sleep.
I dream of glances that might meet,
A chance to play, even just once, so sweet.
A match beneath the fading light,
Night walks after, ice cream in sight.
Your jersey, your socks, little things I hold,
Moments imagined, in stories untold.
💛
I speak your name into the night,
A quiet prayer that feels so right.
You visit dreams I can't explain,
Like gentle, unexpected rain.
Your eyes, your name, your different way,
Are thoughts that never go away.
Though logic says this makes no sense,
My heart builds its own defense.
• • •

Some dreams are too precious for daylight,
so they wait for the stars to appear.

Where football meets poetry under the night sky
- a dreamer under the same stars

Some feelings need no reason, they just exist

- written with love and a football heart
